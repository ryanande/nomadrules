## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Dedicated B2B/partner page
The marketing site SHALL provide a dedicated business page distinct from the consumer pages, targeting B2B/partner audiences (mail forwarding services, RV-friendly attorneys, tax preparers, RV parks).

#### Scenario: Partner visitor reaches the business page
- **WHEN** a visitor navigates to the business page
- **THEN** partner-focused messaging and personas are shown, separate from the consumer landing content

### Requirement: White-label API value proposition
The business page SHALL explain the white-label API offering available on the Business tier — embedding NomadRules law-change data in partner products under the partner's brand.

#### Scenario: Visitor learns about the API
- **WHEN** a visitor views the business page
- **THEN** the white-label API value proposition and example partner use cases are described

### Requirement: Partner contact call to action
The business page SHALL provide a contact/partner call to action so a prospective partner can initiate contact.

#### Scenario: Partner initiates contact
- **WHEN** a visitor clicks the "partner with us" / contact call to action
- **THEN** a contact mechanism (form submission or contact route) is presented and a submission is acknowledged
